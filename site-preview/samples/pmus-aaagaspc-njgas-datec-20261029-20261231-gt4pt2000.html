New Jersey gas price at year end on Polymarket US

This market will settle to Yes if the New Jersey average price of regular gas is greater than $4.2000 for December 31, 2026. Outcome sourced from AAA. This market settles based on the New Jersey average gas prices published daily by AAA at https://gasprices.aaa.com/?state=NJ. Each date's price is used as published by AAA at 10:00 AM ET on that date, provided the price has been updated for that date. If the price has not been updated at that time, the first price updated for that date will be used. If the price for the specified date has not been published within 3 calendar days (ET) of that date, this market will settle to No. For full rules, see: https://polymarketexchange.com/files/product
