Francesco Maestrelli vs Sumit Nagal (atp), November 12 on Polymarket US

This market will settle to the winner of the Francesco Maestrelli vs Sumit Nagal ATP match scheduled for November 12, 2026 at 3:00AM ET (as of the listing of the event's first instrument). If the match does not begin due to a walkover, withdrawal, or cancellation, the market will settle to the last fair market price. If a player retires or is disqualified during the match, the market will settle to the official winner of the match. If the match is delayed, postponed, suspended or otherwise rescheduled to a date and time not within two days of the originally scheduled date and time, the market will settle to the last fair market price.
