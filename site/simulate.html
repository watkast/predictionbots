Why the curve only goes one way

The market is already the forecast

A 50¢ contract is the crowd saying 50%. To beat it you need information the crowd doesn't have, every time, by more than the fee. Most people's "edge" is a feeling about a chart. Slide the win rate up a few points and see how much better than the market you would have to be, consistently, just to stand still.

The fee is the only certainty

Wins and losses average out. The fee doesn't; it leaves on every trade. That is why the median of hundreds of careers slopes down at the fee rate, and why "I'm up this week" and "I'll be down this year" are both usually true.

Streaks are normal, and expensive

At a 50% win rate, five losses in a row happens to most people every month. The streak isn't the problem; what people do after it is. Switch on "double after a loss" and watch the same market end a career three times faster.

Nobody rides to zero in one go

Real people stop, feel better, and deposit again. The chart is the same; it just has pauses in it. If you want to play anyway, read the deal first: what the account asks for, what the bonus really is, and whether we're paid.
