If you are reading this because money is tight tonight and a prediction market looks like the one door that is still open, start here: the door is painted on. A market pays whoever knows the answer, and when nobody knows, it returns your stake minus the fee on average. That is not a judgment about you. It is the arithmetic of the game, and it is the reason the people who run it can afford to advertise.

What follows is the order that works when there is no bandwidth left for a plan. One rung at a time. Write down only the next one.

## Tonight: the emergency

- **Call 211** (or [211.org](https://www.211.org)). It is free, it is local, and it exists for exactly this: rent assistance, utility shut-off help, food, shelter, prescriptions. The person answering has a list you don't.
- **Food:** [Feeding America's food bank finder](https://www.feedingamerica.org/find-your-local-foodbank). No paperwork at most pantries. SNAP applications go through your state and can be expedited within seven days in hardship.
- **Utilities:** [LIHEAP](https://www.acf.hhs.gov/ocs/energy-assistance) pays heating and cooling bills through your state agency; most utilities also have a hardship plan if you call before the shut-off date, not after.
- **Rent:** ask 211 for emergency rental assistance in your county; many programs pay the landlord directly. If an eviction notice has arrived, legal aid is free for most incomes: [LawHelp.org](https://www.lawhelp.org).

None of these charge a fee. Anything that does, tonight, is not help.

## This week: real money

A prediction market is the most expensive way to turn $50 into $100 and the most common way to turn it into $0. Things that pay actual dollars inside a week, with the honest hourly range people report after costs:

- Driving or delivery (Uber, Lyft, DoorDash, Instacart): same-day cashout, roughly $12 to $25 an hour after fuel and wear, depending on city and hours.
- Selling what you already own (Marketplace, eBay, Craigslist): the fastest money most people have, nothing to learn.
- Task and labor apps (TaskRabbit, Thumbtack): $20 to $50 an hour for moving, assembly, cleaning.
- Research studies and user tests (Prolific, UserTesting): $8 to $20 an hour, paid weekly. Small, and positive.
- Your own employer: ask about an advance on earned wages before a payday lender does it for 400% a year.

Skip anything that needs a deposit first, pays under $3 an hour, or calls itself a "trading bot."

## This month: the debt

- **Talk to a nonprofit credit counselor.** Agencies in the [National Foundation for Credit Counseling](https://www.nfcc.org) do a first session free, are licensed in your state, and can set up a debt management plan with your creditors at lower rates. This is the legitimate version of what the ads sell.
- **The one-line scam test:** anyone who wants money up front to forgive, settle or fix your debt, promises a result, or tells you to stop paying creditors and pay them instead, is the scam. The Federal Trade Commission's [debt relief guidance](https://consumer.ftc.gov/articles/how-get-out-debt) says the same in more words.
- **Order of payments when you can't pay everything:** housing, utilities, food, transportation to work, then everything else. A credit card company can wait a month; a landlord's notice has a date on it.
- **Call the creditor before the due date.** Hardship programs, deferrals and reduced minimums exist and are rarely offered unless asked.

## The part nobody puts on a flyer

Money pressure narrows thinking. Researchers who measured it found that being preoccupied with scarcity costs about as much cognitive bandwidth as losing a night's sleep (Mullainathan and Shafir, *Scarcity*, 2013). That is why the bet looks smart at 11 p.m. and why long plans fail under pressure. The fix is not willpower; it is fewer decisions. One rung a day. Write the next one down. Tell one person.

If the app has become part of the problem, **1-800-GAMBLER** (call or text, free, confidential, 24 hours) has heard every version of this and will not judge you; [help in your state](https://www.ncpgambling.org/help-treatment/help-by-state/) is often free. If things feel darker than money, call or text **988**.
