There are two Polymarkets, and most of what you read online is about the other one.

## Two platforms with one name

**Polymarket US** is a U.S. exchange: you sign up with an ID and a selfie, deposit dollars by card, bank transfer or wire, and trade contracts regulated by the Commodity Futures Trading Commission. Polymarket built it by buying QCX LLC, a CFTC-designated contract market, and QC Clearing LLC, a CFTC-registered derivatives clearing organization, in 2025 for a reported $112 million; CFTC staff issued a no-action letter on reporting rules for the two entities on 3 September 2025, and the company said it had the green light to operate in the U.S.

**polymarket.com** is the international, crypto-native market: wallets on a public blockchain, USDC, no U.S. customers. The on-chain studies you see quoted (wallet leaderboards, "84% of wallets lose") describe that book. Polymarket US publishes no account-level data.

## Safe from the company: regulated, segregated, uninsured

- **Oversight.** A designated contract market and a clearing organization each answer to the CFTC: rulebooks filed with the regulator, surveillance duties (the CFTC reminded every platform of them in February and March 2026), audits.
- **Your money.** Exchange customer funds are held through the clearing organization under CFTC customer-protection rules, apart from the company's own money. There is no FDIC insurance and no insurance fund behind a balance.
- **Getting it out.** Withdrawals go back to the method you deposited with, in deposit order: card and ACH in about 3 to 4 business days, wires in about 1. No venue fee to deposit or withdraw. Promotional credit has to be traded before it can be withdrawn. [The details, and what "pending" usually means.](/learn/can-i-withdraw-from-polymarket-us.html)

## Safe from the law: contested for sports

Wisconsin's attorney general sued Polymarket, Kalshi and others in April 2026 to stop sports event contracts in that state; other states have warned or sued over the same contracts. A ruling can close or geofence markets you hold positions in. Your funds stay yours under the rules above; the markets on offer can change.

## Safe from the market: no

The fee schedule changed on 6 November 2026: an ordinary account now pays 9.5% × price × (1 − price) per share to take a price, 37% more than before, while the largest accounts and a new account's first week keep 6.95%. [The schedule at every price.](/learn/polymarket-us-fees.html) A two-sided market returns your stake minus that fee on average, and our own measurements of popular rules on Polymarket US's markets [lost 9 cents per dollar](/posts/2026-11-08-the-45-70-rule-on-689-markets.html).

**Bottom line.** Polymarket US is a regulated exchange, not a crypto casino with a U.S. flag; your deposit is about as protected from the company as money on a U.S. derivatives exchange gets. It is not protected from the fee, from a state closing a market, or from someone who knows more than you. [Your own numbers](/reality.html) · [what a month looks like](/simulate.html?venue=pmus).
