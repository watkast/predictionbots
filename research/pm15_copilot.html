Data

Everything saves on this device. Settlement = the average of the 60 one-second index prints in the last minute before the bell (Polymarket US rules; verified on recorded settlements to $0.005). With the relay the page reads that index series straight from the exchange: the PM line, the final price, the next target and the basis vs Coinbase are exact. Without it they are modeled from Coinbase plus a basis offset. The record pace comes from Coinbase 1-min (5 d), 5-min (30 d) and 15-min (120 d) history, rescanned every 6 h and bumped live; "bad minute" = the chosen percentile of 1-minute adverse moves over those 5 days.
