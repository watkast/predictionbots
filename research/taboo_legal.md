### 1b. Creator/agency SaaS economics

A "chatter scam" class action (*N.Z. v. Verdict: chat automation = platform-ToS violation + undisclosed impersonation → **out** on two hard lines. Scheduling/analytics/tax tools are legal but commoditised (same $15–40 price band) and need the creator's session cookies (ToS-grey). Below the bar.

Buyers: small adult sites, creator platforms, dating, gaming, forums; also non-adult platforms hit by NC/UT/TX social-media laws. ## 2. MMA / UFC and combat sports

### 2a. Market-efficiency evidence (is there a taker edge?)
* Robbins, *Weak Form Efficiency in Sports Betting Markets* (6,190 UFC bouts, 2007–2022, BestFightOdds): average return **−5.82 %** vs 4.40 % margin; slight-underdog bands +2.44/+2.49 % at p≈0.063–0.065 (not significant) ([ArticleGateway](https://articlegateway.com/index.php/AJM/article/view/6051), summarised by [AgentMMA](https://agentmma.com/mma-lab/ufc-public-betting-bias)) [A].
* Miller & Nichols 2026 (peer-reviewed, Tapology data; 2,122 numbered-card bouts UFC 100–293): all-favourites **−4.68 %**, all-underdogs **−9.99 %**, no broad favourite-longshot bias, "few statistically significant positive OOS returns", MMA "largely efficient" ([AgentMMA summary](https://agentmma.com/mma-lab/accurate-ufc-model-lose-money-betting)) [A]. A FightTracker arXiv preprint claiming an edge was **withdrawn** after a longer sample [A].
* Practitioner "edges" are unsignificant small-n: 487 fights 2019–24, "overlooked veteran" +150/+280 bucket +23 % ROI with no tests, favourite ROI not reported ([dev.to/edgelab](https://dev.to/edgelab/ufc-underdog-roi-i-tracked-500-fights-to-find-systematic-mispricings-4khf)) [D]. Action Network 2021: following line moves 54 % but −$1,115 flat [B].
* Conclusion: **no reproducible OOS taker edge in MMA from striking stats or judge models.** That is the pro-maker finding: a market that is efficient at the close but retail-heavy intraday rewards the quoter.

### 2b. Venues, liquidity, fees
* Kalshi: UFC averaged **$2,903,762/day** Jan 1–Apr 20 2026 (NBA $69.2M, PGA $11.6M, F1 $141k), UFC 327 day **$45.0M** ([CasinoBeats](https://casinobeats.com/2026/04/23/weather-markets-ufc-generate-similar-trading-volumes-kalshi-sports-still-dominate/)) [B]. UFC 328 Strickland–Chimaev winner market: **$51.8M notional, $19.0M cash, 280k trades**, $1.2M traded in the final four minutes ([DeFiRate](https://defirate.com/news/kalshis-52-million-ufc-event-had-11-1-million-on-chimaev/)) [B]. Market types per fight: winner `KXUFCFIGHT`, method `KXUFCMOV`, distance `KXUFCDISTANCE`, rounds `KXUFCROUNDS`, `KXUFCMOF` ([kalshi.com/ufc/ufc-316](https://kalshi.com/ufc/ufc-316)) [A]. Fees: standard sports formula (taker 0.07·P·(1−P), maker 0.0175·P·(1−P) ≈ 0.44¢ at 50¢, see `configs/ventures.yaml`); no UFC-specific override on the [market-specific fees page](https://help.kalshi.com/getting-started/market-specific-fees) [A].
* Polymarket: 17 settled UFC markets **$27.4M**; main-card fights $1–4.5M each (Aspinall–Gane $3.2M, Du Plessis–Chimaev $4.5M), prelims ~$1M; even novelty "touch gloves?" $117k ([Polyguana](https://polyguana.com/resolved/entities/ufc)) [B]. US access only via Polymarket US (see `pm_poly_mm`).
* Novig: docs list no MMA series as of the Oct-2026 check in `sports_exchange_maker_novig`; recheck. PrizePicks/Underdog list UFC "fantasy score" and significant-strike props — covered by `niche_edges.md` #6 (human places, limits arrive) — bots are a ToS violation there.
* Boxing/BKFC/Power Slap/WWE: Kalshi lists celebrity boxing (Manziel–Menery 1.5M contracts, Ray J 1.2M) and one-off WWE event contracts (McMahon return, Rousey); Polymarket lists Logan Paul WWE markets; **no recurring BKFC/Power Slap series found**; WWE outcomes are scripted (insider risk, likely why platforms avoid match winners) ([OctagonAI](https://www.octagonai.co/markets/sports/boxing/), [TheLines](https://www.thelines.com/prediction-markets/sports/wwe/)) [B]. Retail prices on celebrity boxing are probably soft but each market is a one-off with no sharp reference line — skip.

Our existing recorder + Odds API key gives a **closing-line vs exchange-mid dataset** nobody sells — that is lane 3 (combat data).
* Pick/newsletter SaaS: one Subtext case study — "MMA Shark" **$109/mo SMS picks, >$22k gross/mo**, no record published ([Subtext](https://info.joinsubtext.com/mma_shark_case_study)) [C]. * Gym B2B: Zen Planner $99–289/mo, Gymdesk $75–200/mo ([Gymdesk](https://gymdesk.com/blog/zen-planner-review)) [C]; saturated vertical SaaS, no agent angle.
* Fighter-data licensing: UFC/Zuffa own Fight Pass stats; no third-party licensing programme found → skip.

* **Federal:** state-licensed *medical* cannabis moved to Schedule III by an April-22-2026 order; banking (BSA/AML) unchanged, non-medical remains a federal crime, SAFE/SAFER Banking not yet reintroduced in the Senate as of May-2026 ([Cannabis Business Times](https://www.cannabisbusinesstimes.com/business-issues-benchmarks/safe-banking-act/news/15825992/safe-banking-act-nowhere-to-be-found-in-wake-of-schedule-iii-cannabis-order)) [A/B]. * **Metrc:** Metrc Connect "Standard" API tier is **free** (portal, docs, email support), custom tier "monthly pricing based on needs"; 28 jurisdictions incl. Colorado ([Metrc Connect](https://www.metrc.com/track-and-trace-technology/metrc-connect/)) [C]; a competing vendor estimates integrations at "$100k+" ([Flourish](https://www.flourishsoftware.com/blog/the-true-cost-of-metrc)) [C, biased]. Compliance reporting is dominated by Dutchie/Flourish/Treez/Bamboo; a solo tool would be a reconciliation/alerts add-on sold to a handful of Colorado licensees — plausible but slow and sales-heavy (bot share ≈50 %).

THCA ≤0.3 % dry weight and final products **≤0.4 mg total THC per container**, effective **2026-11-12**; CBD/industrial hemp survive but cannot be food/supplements or carry health claims ([Harris Sliwoski](https://harris-sliwoski.com/cannalawblog/notes-on-the-federal-prohibition-of-intoxicating-hemp/), [Vicente](https://vicentellp.com/insights/2026-federal-hemp-ban-what-it-means-for-the-future-of-consumable-hemp-products/)) [A]. **THCa/D8 automation = federally illegal in four weeks → out.** CBD affiliate: CBDistillery 20–35 % + 20 % recurring, Healthworx 40 %, Social CBD 20 % ([LinkClicky](https://linkclicky.com/affiliate-programs/cbd/)) [C]; Google/Meta still restrict CBD ads, so traffic is SEO-only (dead per `non_trading_ventures.md`).

Pouches are "other tobacco products" under state law in most states. **Out.** A pouch/vape *price-tracker* is legal but the affiliate programmes are thin and ad platforms ban nicotine — below bar.

* **Firearms accessories / ammo:** Stripe, PayPal, Square prohibit firearms sales; **accessories (holsters, optics, cleaning) "no restrictions, standard processing"**, ammo "generally processable" with state compliance, magazines need geo-blocks ([Convesio](https://convesio.com/knowledgebase/article/firearms-payment-processing/)) [B]. ## 5. Debt / credit / legal-adjacent

* **Medical-bill negotiation:** fee-on-savings is standard and unlicensed: Goodbill 20 % capped $1,000; CareRoute 18–25 % capped $1,000; Resolve 10–25 % + $249–499 refundable deposit, $5k minimum; Dollar For (nonprofit) free charity-care applications ([CareRoute comparison](https://www.careroute.ai/bill-defense/compare-medical-bill-negotiation-services), [Goodbill FAQ](https://help.goodbill.com/en/articles/254209)) [C]. Mechanics: HIPAA authorization from the patient, itemized bill (UB-04/CPT), error scan, financial-assistance policy lookup (501(r) hospitals must publish), negotiation letters/calls. Bot share ~50 % (phone work is human); customer acquisition is the hard part. Lane 5 — build only as a co-pilot for the owner's own bills first.
* **Unclaimed property (Colorado):** finder fee **≤10 %**, agreements unenforceable for **24 months** after delivery to the Treasurer, written/signed, must state the owner can claim free (CRS 38-13-1301–1304) ([ungovr CO](https://www.ungovr.org/unclaimed/co)) [A]. No Colorado finder licence, but IL/NC/CA require licences/bonds (`agent_services.md` §7). 10 % of small claims after a 2-year wait, plus outreach that reads as spam → **out** (confirms the earlier memo).
* **Foreclosure surplus funds (Colorado):** CRS 38-38-111(2.5)(c): *"An agreement to pay compensation to recover or assist in recovering an amount due to the borrower [from the public trustee] is not enforceable"* and inducing one is a **class 2 misdemeanor**; unclaimed overbids go to the state after 6 months ([CRS 38-38-111](https://colorado.public.law/statutes/crs_38-38-111)) [A]. **Out.**
* **Class-action claims for others:** third-party filers are tolerated only in securities cases with explicit court orders (e.g., the Payment Card settlement's TPF order and monthly TPF reports at [paymentcardsettlement.com](https://www.PaymentCardSettlement.com/Content/Documents/New%20Docs/Order%20Regarding%20Third-Party%20Claims%20Filing%20Services.pdf)); administrators run duplicate/bot detection ([Kroll](https://www.kroll.com/en/publications/settlement-administration/avoiding-risks-with-third-party-claims-filers)) [A/B]. Filing consumer claims for others is not allowed without authority and courts police it; a **claim-aggregation data product** (open settlements, deadlines, eligibility rules as an API/newsletter) is legal and already crowded (ClassAction.org, Top Class Actions). Below bar.
* **Credit repair:** CROA 15 U.S.C. §1679b(b) bans any fee before services are "fully performed"; written contract, 3-day cancel; TSR 6-month rule for phone sales; Lexington Law/CreditRepair.com **$2.7B** CFPB judgment, Chapter 11 ([getoutofdebt](https://getoutofdebt.org/265926/credit-repair-upfront-fee-illegal)) [A]. Selling dispute-letter automation to consumers is a credit repair organization in substance → **out**.
* **Ticket/parking appeals, demand letters:** FTC v. DoNotPay ($193k, final Feb-2025) prohibits unsupported "replaces a lawyer" claims, not document automation ([FTC](https://www.ftc.gov/node/86371)) [A]. Colorado's OARC adopted a 3-year **non-prosecution policy** for legal-help tech developers with lawyer supervision and non-lawyer disclosure (Sep-2025); Texas statutorily excluded software from UPL ([EDRM](https://edrm.net/2026/03/colorado-policy-could-shield-ai-from-complaints-regarding-unauthorized-practice-of-law/)) [B]. Legal information + templates = fine; advising a specific person which claim to make = UPL. Lane 7; tiny ARPU ($5–20 one-offs) and DoNotPay-sized incumbents.

## 6. Death / estate and other awkward niches

* **Obituary-driven estate-sale / probate leads:** probate court records are public; vendors sell leads from **$65+** to attorneys ([CounselStack](https://www.counselstack.com/legal-leads/probate)) [C]. Legal, but the product is "contact the bereaved first"; TCPA/CAN-SPAM apply to outreach and reputational cost is total. **Out on the exploitative line.**
* **Digital-legacy services:** Everplans/Cake/Beyondly are subscription vaults ($40–100/yr); no distribution edge, heavy trust/security burden. Below bar.
* **Pet-cremation lead gen:** Google Ads agencies for funeral homes exist ([PartingPro](https://marketing.partingpro.com/index.html)) [D]; this is local lead-gen (rank-and-rent, scored 4/10 in `agent_services.md`), nothing specific to pets. Below bar.
* **Bail bonds / bounty data:** Colorado bail recovery agents are licensed (CRS 16-3-… ; DORA), data brokering for them needs FCRA/GLBA-permissible purpose. **Out.**
* **Repo / skip-tracing:** GLBA, DPPA and FCRA permissible-purpose regimes; FTC data-broker FCRA actions (Spokeo, TruthFinder/Instant Checkmate $5.8M) ([Hunton](https://www.hunton.com/privacy-and-information-security-law/ftc-announces-settlement-data-brokers-alleged-fcra-violations), [ERE](https://www.ere.net/articles/ftc-warns-background-screeners-for-possible-privacy-violations)) [A]. **Out.**
* **Background-check reselling:** operating or reselling makes you a consumer reporting agency under FCRA (accuracy, dispute, permissible-purpose duties) — same sources. **Out.**
