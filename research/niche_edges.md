## Rejected with evidence

- **Electricity demand response / VPP (Colorado):** Xcel Renewable Battery Connect pays $250/kW upfront (≤$5,000) + **$100/yr** for 60 three-hour events — needs a $10–15k battery; Xcel EV Smart V2G is invitation-only (~$250/yr); Tesla VPP $17.55/event in TX. Median US VPP household earns $120–180/yr [C]. OhmConnect is CA-only in practice. Not an edge, a rebate.
- **Griddy-style wholesale retail:** Texas HB 16 (2021) banned residential wholesale-indexed plans after Uri $10k+ bills; Colorado has no retail electric choice at all. Dead.
- **Carbon/RECs/water:** Xpansiv CBL is institutional; retail exposure is KRBN/GRN beta; voluntary credits have no retail exit. Colorado has no SREC market (Xcel Solar*Rewards is a production payment). C-BT water units ≈$85k each (peak $101k 2022), broker-settled, not automatable.
- **Commodity seasonal spreads:** arXiv 2609.12227 (15 futures, 2016–24 OOS): no seasonal model beats equal-weight after 8.6 bp costs; SSA median path −11%; none of 18 Sharpe tests significant after Holm. Nat-gas "widowmaker" (Mar/Apr) is a storage-theory story with no retail-size OOS evidence; CME micro NG does not exist (only full/e-mini). USDA WASDE: corn reacts within the announcement minute, abnormal vol ~51 min, no drift tested [A].
- **Weather derivatives retail:** none; CME weather futures are dealer-only in practice. Kalshi weather ladders already covered.
- **Dividend capture:** Henry & Koski 2017: +0.405% pre-cost, +0.24% only for high-execution-skill institutions; retail loses the 20–40 bp to spreads and the 60-day qualified-dividend rule [A].
- **Russell reconstitution / index effects:** S&P 500 inclusion AR 7.6% (1990s) → 0.8% (2010s, n.s.), deletions −16.6% → −0.6% ("disappearing index effect"); Russell went semi-annual in 2026 (June/Dec); 2025 close printed $217B — HFT horizon [A].
- **Spin-off forced selling:** long-run spin-off premium is in the 1990s–2000s samples; 2020s results not significant in replications; needs 6–12-mo holds and is beta-heavy.
- **Merger appraisal arbitrage:** dead after DFC (2017), Dell (2017), Aruba (2019) — courts appraise at or below deal price [A/legal].
- **Dutch-auction tenders without odd-lot clause:** Expensify 2026 ($0.98–1.20, $25M) type deals pay only the odd-lot slice; no systematic edge in the Dutch range itself.
- **Pre-IPO platforms (user's SourceDay shares):** these are exit venues, not edges. Hiive ≈5–6.8% fee, $25k min; EquityZen ≈2.5%, $5–10k min post Feb-2026 restructure, ROFR pre-cleared; Forge 2–5% seller-paid, $100k min direct. Expect 20–40% discounts to last round for non-AI names and 1–3 months to close.
- **SPAC warrants:** 2025–26 SPAC vintage warrants trade at option value on deal completion; no retail-size edge beyond the trust arb already covered.
- **Treasury auction tail:** single-price auctions show no significant auction-vs-WI gap (Malvey & Archibald; NY Fed CI 11-2); noncompetitive bidders get the stop-out yield — worth 0–2 bp.
- **I-bond gift box:** locks the 0.90% fixed rate for future-year delivery but needs a second person, counts against their $10k on delivery, locks cash; value ≈ (0.9% − future fixed) × $10k ≈ $0–50/yr. HSA/529 covered in yield_vehicles.md.
- **Margin-rate arbitrage via short box:** box borrow 4.6–5.1% > T-bill 4.2–4.5% → negative carry; only useful to replace IBKR margin (~5.8% Pro <$100k) for a strategy that needs leverage; needs portfolio margin for clean treatment.
- **FDA/PDUFA run-up:** only vendor claims (BiopharmaWatch "20–40% run-up", unsourced); no post-2023 net-of-cost study; binary-event tails.
- **Earnings-call/FOMC NLP, Reddit/X sentiment:** arXiv 2507.22922 (WSB, GME/AMC): sentiment weakly correlated; comment volume and Google trends stronger; no net-of-cost strategy. Post-2021 academic replications find no retail-latency edge.
- **Sporttrade:** exited NJ/AZ/CO/IA/VA on 2026-06-26; CFTC DCM+DCO application pending since Feb-2026. Betfair US-blocked. No esports exchange beyond Kalshi/Polymarket.
- **DFS optimization bots:** DraftKings ToU bans "automated means… creating a lineup, editing a lineup"; scripting banned since 2016; multi-entry caps. Underdog/PrizePicks likewise.
- **Horse racing (Benter-style):** CAW is ~30% of US handle ($3.3B of $11.04B 2025) with rebates and batch-bet access retail cannot get; NYRA now blocks CAW batches at 1 min to post. Retail faces 15–25% takeout with no rebate — a Benter model needs the rebate to clear it. Colorado ADW (TwinSpires/FanDuel Racing) has no public API.
- **Jai-alai, chess betting:** no US exchange liquidity; skip.
- **CME-vs-Coinbase basis:** BasisWatch is login-gated; crypto memo already found CME basis ≈ T-bill in 2026; micro BTC ($0.1 BTC) round-trip commissions + Coinbase spot fees exceed the residual.
- **Coinbase Advanced maker rebates:** none below VIP (entry tier 0.50% maker / 0.90% taker from $10k volume; rebates ≤0.4 bp only on international perps). Kraken maker 0.25%/0.16% at <$10k is the cheaper US venue for small makers.
- **Staking vs restaking:** Coinbase ETH ~1.7% / SOL ~3.4% net already covered; EigenLayer restaking via cbETH adds slashing/smart-contract risk for ~0–2% extra. **NFT floor-sweep bots:** Blur $1.43B/mo (Mar-2023) → <$7M/mo (Mar-2026), frontend shutting Nov-2026. Dead.

## Implementation notes for the three that clear the bar
- **brokerage_bonus_roll:** bot = scheduled scraper of DoC brokerage-bonus page + Robinhood/Moomoo/Public/Tastytrade terms PDFs → `data/bonus_offers.json`; tracker of hold clocks/clawback windows per account; alert when a clock clears and a ≥1% offer is live. Human: ACATS, Gold subscription, tax form. Keep assets in USFR so the roll is yield-neutral.
- **oddlot_tender_scanner:** EDGAR full-text search API (`efts.sec.gov/LATEST/search-index?q="odd lot"&forms=SC TO-I,SC TO-T,S-4`) every 6 h; parse offer price/range, expiry, odd-lot clause; compare to last trade (Alpaca/Polygon); emit expected $ = 99 × (price − ask) − fee; threshold $75. Human: buy 99 sh, submit tender election.
- **stablecoin_depeg_catcher:** Kraken USDC/USD + USDT/USD resting bids ladder (0.97/0.95/0.93) sized to idle collateral; cancel/replace daily; sell ladder 0.995–1.000 after fill; hard rule: cancel all bids if the issuer halts redemptions (Circle/Tether status page poll).
