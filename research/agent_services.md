# Agents selling services to businesses and to other agents — evidence audit (2026-10-17)

Owner: solo US operator, Claude Code agents on GitHub Actions + a Mac mini, $1k–$20k. Question: is there a *new* bot-run revenue lane in "agent sells a service to an SMB or to another agent", beyond what `non_trading_ventures.md` and `../ventures/saas/PLAN.md` already cover (micro-SaaS base rates, Apify/RapidAPI fees, bug bounty, KDP/YouTube/Etsy, SEO, churning).

Grades: **A** platform/regulator/court disclosure or verified payment data (Stripe-synced TrustMRR, on-chain audit) · **B** credible practitioner data with method · **C** vendor/marketing or self-reported · **D** anecdote, modelled or unsourced.
"$/mo" = net to the operator after platform fees and API cost. First-dollar = calendar time from zero to the first paid invoice if the owner does the human steps promptly.

## 1. Agent-to-agent commerce

### 1a. Paid MCP servers and MCP marketplaces
* **Directories do not pay.** Smithery, Glama, mcp.so, PulseMCP and the Anthropic/OpenAI connector directories list servers; none publishes a payout program or revenue share (composio comparison pages, [Glama alternatives](https://composio.dev/content/glama-alternatives), [Smithery alternatives](https://composio.dev/content/smithery-alternative)) [B]. Anthropic's Claude marketplace revenue terms were "unpublished as of April 2026" ([AgentMarketCap](https://agentmarketcap.ai/blog/2026/04/08/agent-creator-economy-developer-revenue-gpt-store-claude-marketplace)) [C].
* **The only working rail is Apify pay-per-event.** Apify keeps 20 % and passes platform cost ([docs](https://docs.apify.com/academy/actor-marketing-playbook/store-basics/how-actor-monetization-works)) [A]; MCP servers can be published as Actors with `Actor.charge()` events, "zero cost to publish" ([apify.com/mcp/developers](https://apify.com/mcp/developers)) [A]. Apify's own banners: "$1.7M paid out last month", "many developers earn over $3k", a testimonial at "$2,000+/mo" [C]. Counter-evidence from a practitioner: a Content-to-Social MCP at $0.07/transformation had **zero paying users two weeks after launch**; "modest single server ≈ $500/mo", top-tier $3–10k/mo "per MCPize internal reporting" ([godberrystudios](https://godberrystudios.com/posts/how-to-monetize-mcp-servers-2026/)) [C/D]. 21st.dev Magic's "$10k MRR in six weeks" is an unverified founder claim [D].
* Observed price points for MCP/agent tools: $0.01/call with 100 free; $0.02–0.05 per successful enrichment match; Ref $0.009/credit; $9–20/mo unlimited tiers; $49–199/mo data feeds [C].
* **Saturation:** 89k Apify Actors (see saas PLAN); 172,559 x402 tool listings from 3,241 sellers ([agent402 economy](https://agent402.tools/economy)) [B].

### 1b. x402 / Coinbase agentic payments, Stripe MPP, agent wallets
* Headline: 138M x402 payments on Base in the 12 months to 2026-07-01, 87 % between $0.0001 and $0.10; median funding-to-first-payment 18 min; Chainalysis declines to publish a dollar total ([crypto.news on Chainalysis](https://crypto.news/ai-agents-made-138-million-crypto-payments-what-did-they-buy/), [aiweekly](https://aiweekly.co/alerts/chainalysis-base-x402-agentic-payments-hit-138m-in-q3-with-87-under-10-cents)) [B].
* **Real dollars are tiny.** a16z's Levine: ~$1.6M of agent payments in 30 days after removing wash trades, >90 % below the $24M headline; buyers are developers trying Firecrawl ($0.01/query), Browserbase, Freepik ([Cointelegraph, 2026-03-12](https://regional-front.cointelegraph.com/news/ai-agent-payment-volume-closer-to-1-6m-says-a16z)) [B]. Bitquery Aug-2026 audit: 54,383 Base addresses received a payment, **median seller earned $2.50**; ~1,300 sellers took ≥10 payments in July; a one-cent Polygon loop produced 5.7M "payments" for $57k ([Bitquery](https://bitquery.io/investigations/x402-ai-agent-payments-audit)) [B]. agent402's ledger: 3,241 sellers, 9,933 paid settlements from **285 distinct outside buyers** in the last 30 days — roughly 3 settlements per seller per month [B].
* **Stripe Machine Payments Protocol** (2026-03-18): any Stripe account accepts agent payments via PaymentIntents; settles to the normal Stripe balance; launch sellers Browserbase, Parallel, PostalForm; no fee schedule or volume published ([Stripe blog](https://stripe.com/it/blog/machine-payments-protocol)) [A for mechanics, – for volume]. This matters for us only as a **zero-marginal-cost extra checkout** on an API we would sell anyway.
* Coinbase x402 Bazaar is a discovery index, no fees stated, no seller counts ([Coinbase](https://www.coinbase.com/fr/developer-platform/discover/launches/x402-bazaar)) [C].
* Virtuals ACP: "$470M agent GDP, 1.77M jobs" are self-reported, incentive-funded ($1M/mo), token revenue −96 % Jan–Jun 2025 ([Forkast](https://forkast.news/the-machine-as-buyer-virtuals-acp-and-the-evaluator-problem/)) [C]. Moltbook/RentAHuman: no USD figures anywhere; viral posts largely human-prompted ([Bitfinity](https://www.blog.bitfinity.network/openclaw-moltbook-and-how-ai-agents-are-becoming-employers-in-2026/)) [D]. **Not a market.**
* Legal/ToS: x402 settles USDC on Base; receiving stablecoins for a service is ordinary income, no MSB issue for the seller; Coinbase facilitator ToS applies. MPP is plain Stripe (KYC once, see saas PLAN §g).

**Verdict:** add MPP + x402 + Apify-MCP as *checkouts* on the oddsmath/odds-feed APIs (lane 1). Expect single-digit buyers per month; it costs an afternoon of agent time.

### 1c. Selling datasets to AI labs / data marketplaces
* Datacurve/Shipd (coding data, $15M Series A Oct-2025) pays 14k **vetted human engineers** per output-based bounty; automated test + human reviewer gate ([Sacra](https://sacra.com/c/datacurve)) [B]. Scale/Outlier/Mercor-type work is human labour with anti-AI-assist policies; **not bot-runnable and out of scope.**
* Marketplaces open to individuals: **DataVendor** (invite-reviewed, free listing, seller keeps 80 %); Opendatabay requires a registered company ([aitrainer.work](https://aitrainer.work/sell/datasets)) [C]. Datarade lists vendor products (UniCourt PACER feeds etc.) but is B2B-vendor oriented [C]. Free Law Project charges $170–750/h for PACER bulk-data consulting to for-profits and counts OpenAI among clients ([free.law](https://free.law/data-consulting/)) [A] — a signal that *curated public-record* data has buyers, but FLP's moat is years of PACER spend.
* What buyers pay for: "private, domain-specific, documented data … a few thousand expert-labelled rows with a clear license"; scraped public data and downloaded datasets are explicitly not wanted [C]. No per-row price prints exist for a niche like ours.
* **What we legally own:** the multi-venue recorder's tick/tape/book snapshots, de-vig outputs, weather-ensemble calibration tables, paper-trade logs. **Must check before listing:** Kalshi, Polymarket and Novig API terms on redistribution of market data (Kalshi's API ToS restricts commercial redistribution of "Kalshi Data" — owner/agent to confirm current text); derived statistics (our own de-vigged probabilities, fee-adjusted edges) are safer than raw tape. Court/FOIA data is public domain but PACER has per-page fees and state portals have ToS; Colorado CORA responses can be resold.

**Verdict:** lane 4 — list *derived* datasets (not raw exchange tape) on DataVendor and a Gumroad/Stripe page; price by the row; expect $0 for months; it is a by-product with zero marginal cost.

### Rank-and-rent
* Claim: "Kyle" portfolio ~$100k/mo, $1–2k per ranked site, $1,000/mo after a 7-day free lead trial; author sells courses, no records; sites can "get stuck for three months"; AI picks matched Google's top-3 map pack < 25 % of the time ([ryandoser, 2026-06-16](https://ryandoser.com/rank-and-rent-seo/)) [D]. ### Legal: can outreach be automated?
* **Email (CAN-SPAM):** B2B cold email is legal with truthful headers, sender ID, postal address, working opt-out honoured within 10 business days; Google/Yahoo bulk rules: SPF/DKIM/DMARC, one-click unsubscribe, spam-complaint rate < 0.3 % ([Valley, Aug-2026](https://www.joinvalley.co/blog/compliant-cold-email-gdpr-google-yahoo-rules)) [B]. CAN-SPAM penalties up to ~$53k per email (FTC schedule). A bot can run compliant cold email to *businesses* at low volume from a separate domain; it cannot run it at scale without burning domains.
* **Calls/texts (TCPA):** the one-to-one consent rule was vacated (11th Cir., 2025-01-24) so the standard is prior express written consent for marketing autodials/texts; **AI voices are "artificial voices"** and need the same consent; opt-outs by any reasonable means within 10 business days; revocation-all rule delayed to 2027-01-31; damages $500–1,500 per call/text, uncapped ([compliancehub](https://compliancehub.wiki/tcpa-2026-consent-revocation-one-to-one-rule-vacated-compliance/)) [B]. **Outbound cold SMS/AI-calls to SMB cell phones = no.** Inbound missed-call text-back is transactional (customer initiated) and treated as compliant if it names the business, stays on topic, carries opt-out, and uses a 10DLC-registered sender [C]. State mini-TCPAs (FL, OK, WA) add private rights of action.
* **Unclaimed property finders:** Illinois requires a $500 licence, background check, bond up to $100k, 10 % fee cap, $10k/violation unlicensed; NC, CA similar registration ([IL Treasurer](https://www.illinoistreasurer.gov/?p=11602)) [A]. **Tax-sale overages:** Michigan made MCL 211.78t the exclusive remedy, retroactive, 95 % cap, litigation ongoing ([Law360 2026-07-16](https://www.law360.com/real-estate-authority/articles/2502024)) [A]; most states cap finder fees or require attorneys. **Class actions:** administrators flag bot filings (780k claims vs 18k-person class; 97 % invalid in one case) and the Daily Journal/Gibson Dunn discussion (Oct-2026) centres on fraud detection ([Foley](https://www.foley.com/insights/publications/2024/07/fraud-rising-claims-made-class-action-settlements/), [Gibson Dunn](https://www.gibsondunn.com/chris-chorba-discusses-use-of-ai-agents-in-class-action-settlement-claims-with-daily-journal/)) [A]. Filing one's *own* legitimate claims by bot is fine and worth ~$0–50/yr. **All three recovery lanes rejected.**

## 5. Government / data-driven
* **Federal micro-purchases:** MPT $15,000 (from $10k) and SAT $350,000 effective 2025-10-01 ([SmallGovCon](https://smallgovcon.com/statutes-and-regulations/finalized-increases-to-micro-purchase-simplified-acquisition-and-other-thresholds/)) [A]. Below MPT: no competition requirement, cardholder buys on a Government Purchase Card, pays in 1–3 days; SAM.gov UEI registration is free; "short-form professional services: market-research report, website audit, document translation" are typical; "many first-time vendors win a $2–8k order" is the author's assertion ([SLED.AI, Sep-2026](https://sledai.com/blog/easiest-federal-contracts-under-25k/)) [D]. Mechanism for us: bot watches SAM.gov + state portals (Colorado VSS, BidNet) for sub-$15k data/automation asks, drafts capability statements and quotes, emails small-business specialists; owner signs. Lane 5: lumpy, human relationship-driven, worth a monitor.
* **Court-record data:** CourtListener/RECAP API is free and public-domain; UniCourt sells normalised PACER feeds on Datarade; FLP bills $170–750/h [A]. A solo can build a niche feed (e.g. Colorado county civil filings) but PACER costs $0.10/page and state portals' ToS vary. Folded into lane 4 as a possible second dataset.
* **FOIA data products:** legal, slow (20 working days federal, often months), results are public domain; viable only as inputs to lane 4.

## 6. New in 2026 with evidence of solo earnings
* **Cloudflare pay-per-crawl:** 1M+ zones enabled, 1B+ daily HTTP 402s, most declined; OAI-SearchBot/ChatGPT-User pay, GPTBot/ClaudeBot/Google-Extended mostly skip; general content clears $0.001–0.005 per fetch ([Presenc, Apr-2026](https://presenc.ai/research/cloudflare-pay-per-crawl-state-2026)) [C]; worked example: 1M views/mo → $20–200/mo ([LeakyPaywall](https://leakypaywall.com/cloudflare-pay-per-crawl-income-or-spare-change/)) [D]. Free to switch on for any content/data pages we host; income rounds to zero.
* **Agent-native checkouts (MPP/x402) on an existing API:** the only genuinely new 2026 mechanism, and the data above says it adds a handful of buyers. Worth it only because it is free.
* No Starter-Story / IndieHackers / HN thread in 2026 produced a *verified* solo agent-services income above the TrustMRR voice-agent examples ($0–$1,956 MRR). The "$10k/mo AI agency" genre is entirely modelled or anonymous [D].
