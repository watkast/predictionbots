Post from @predictionbots. No link in tweet 1; link in the last tweet and in a reply.

**1/**
Polymarket US changed its fee schedule on Nov 6.

If you trade under $10,000 a month (almost everyone), your taker fee went from 6.95% × p × (1−p) to 9.5%.

That's +37%. Accounts over $250k/month kept the old rate.

Here's what it costs, in dollars.

Break-even win rate at 50¢: 51.7% → 52.4%

**3/**
The welcome rate hides it. New accounts pay 6.95% for days 1–7. From day 8, tier placement uses the HIGHER of projected or trailing volume, and the ordinary account lands at 9.5%.

Your first week is not your fee.

**4/**
What didn't change: resting orders still pay nothing. That is the only lever a small account controls: post a limit order and wait, and accept it may not fill. Taking the displayed price is where the fee lives.
