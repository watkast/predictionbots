# Venue partner programs — what we know, what we ask, the application drafts

Status 8 Nov 2026. Noah submits once the site is live; Claude drafted. Read the contract for non-disparagement and
creative rules before signing; if the terms conflict with the message, we drop the referral, not the message.

## What we know (verified 8 Nov 2026)

- Kalshi's current publisher offer: "Trade $25, get $25" in trading credit (not withdrawable; 90 days; 18+; not AZ, MN,
  NV, WA). Run by RotoWire, Action Network, Covers via partner codes. Kalshi was actively recruiting affiliates in late
  2025 and runs deals through an affiliate network (unnamed in reporting); a Kalshi "Affiliate / DSP Partnerships
  Manager" role exists, so there is a desk to write to.
- Polymarket US's current offer: "Deposit $10, get $25" (1× playthrough; 18+; not NV; code at sign-up only). Covers runs
  it with the disclosure "we may receive compensation if you sign up through our links."
- Typical economics in this category (iGB Affiliate, Nov 2025): CPA around $100 or less per new trader, sometimes a
  volume deal such as $5 per $1,000 traded in the first 30 days. Sportsbooks pay ~$300 CPA by comparison. No licence is
  required of affiliates as of late 2025; state regulators may change that.
- X policy (Feb 2026): paid-partnership/affiliate accounts may not post gambling content, and Kalshi removed all its X
  affiliate badges on 23 Feb 2026. Consequence for us: the Prediction News X account is editorial only, never a badged
  affiliate account, never carrying referral codes. Referral links live on the site, under the disclosure.
- Our break-even math: at ~$100 CPA, two sign-ups a month cover the AI subscription. At "$5 per $1,000 traded," a
  typical small trader produces very little; CPA is the structure to ask for.

## What we ask for in both conversations

1. CPA per verified, funded first trade (not per click), paid monthly; the rate in writing so we can publish it.
2. Permission to publish the rate and to show the venue's own promotion creative next to our fee and odds figures.
3. Confirmation that factual fee, settlement and probability content about the venue does not breach any
   non-disparagement clause. We will not sign a clause that does.
4. Geo-targeting data or a list of excluded states so the block is hidden where the offer is not permitted.
5. No exclusivity. We measure both venues and will show both offers.

PredictionBots.io is an independent measurement site for prediction markets, covering Kalshi and Polymarket US. We
publish fee calculators built from each venue's published schedule, independent 30-day benchmarks of the trading bots

We would like to join Kalshi's publisher program on a CPA basis. Our approach is "explain, then convert": a reader

the fee at the price they are likely to trade, and a disclosure that Kalshi pays us for the sign-up. We believe this
produces better-informed, longer-lived customers than promo-code pages, and we are happy to share conversion and
retention data to test that.

What we need from you: a CPA rate we can publish, permission to use Kalshi's own creative, and confirmation that
factual content about fees and settlement is compatible with your terms. We will geo-gate the offer to permitted
states and carry responsible-gambling messaging on every page that shows it.
